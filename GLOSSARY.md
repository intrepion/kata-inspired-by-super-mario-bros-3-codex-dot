# Lantern Vale language

**Mica**: The courier who carries the valley's lost light home.

**Lantern**: A level's destination, restored by reaching it. Avoid: flag, goalpost.

**Mote**: An optional golden collectible. Each has a stable identity and can only count once per expedition.

**Moon seal**: An optional blue collectible on an alternate high route.

**Expedition**: One attempt at a trail, from entry through retries to restoration.

**Waystone**: A safe mid-trail place from which Mica returns after falling.

**Sail**: A feather power that slows a falling jump while jump is held.

**Trail**: A campaign level unlocked by restoring the previous lantern.

**Heart**: One unit of protection; a fall retries immediately, an enemy contact consumes one.
